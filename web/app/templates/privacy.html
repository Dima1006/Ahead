What we collect

  • Customers: name, e-mail, phone (optional), password (stored only as a salted hash), and your orders (venue, items, arrival time, note).
  • Venues: owner e-mail, password hash, venue name, address, description, opening hours, menu and photos, and the orders received.
  • Technical data: a session cookie that keeps you signed in, and your IP address, used only for abuse protection (rate limits) and security.
  • Messages you send through the contact form.
  • Waitlist: your e-mail, city and, if you give them, your first name and the coffee shop you would use the service at.

Why we use it

To run the service you asked for: create your account, pass your order to the venue, show its status, and keep the site secure (legal basis: performance of a contract and our legitimate interest in security, Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). We use waitlist details only to see where people want the service and to e-mail you about its launch, based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time. We may tell coffee shops how many people joined, per city or per coffee shop named, without names or e-mail addresses. We send other e-mails only about your account and orders. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Who receives it

The venue you order from sees your name, phone, note and order. Our hosting and e-mail providers process data on our behalf. If you use Telegram notifications or Google sign-in, those services receive the data needed for that feature.

Cookies

We use only essential cookies: the session and your chosen language. Your cart and the sound setting are kept in your browser. No analytics or advertising cookies.

How long

Your account data is kept until you delete the account. After deletion, venues keep order totals without your name, phone or note, for their bookkeeping. Waitlist details are deleted when you ask us to remove you (use the contact above) and in any case after 2 years.

Your rights

You can see and correct your details in My account, download your data there, and delete your account there. You may also ask us to restrict or stop processing, or complain to the supervisory authority (in Poland: UODO, uodo.gov.pl).
